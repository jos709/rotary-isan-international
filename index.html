WHO WE ARE

Rotary ISAN – International is a friendly, English-speaking Rotary satellite club bringing together Thai and international residents in Buriram who want to make a practical contribution to their community. We are part of the Rotary Club of Krasang and Rotary International.

WHAT WE DO

We work with local organisations, schools, hospitals and community groups to identify practical needs and turn good ideas into real projects.

Krasang Hospital

Working with Krasang Hospital to provide four urgently needed infusion pumps and reduce unnecessary patient transfers.

Water Sustainability

Supporting sustainable water and energy improvements at Anuban Krasang School.

Wheelchair project photo

Wheelchair Assistance

Helping improve mobility and independence for people in our local community.

Books for Kids photo

Books for Kids

Supporting children's English learning through the distribution of quality illustrated books.

GET INVOLVED

You don't need to have been a Rotarian before. You simply need an interest in helping your community.
